Every card carries the nearest Metro line and station. That is the closest station to the venue's area rather than a door-to-door route — Hauz Khas Village is a genuine walk from Hauz Khas station, and most Gurugram venues off the Yellow Line still need an auto at the end. Confirm price and timing on the linked page before you travel; small venues change schedules constantly, and Delhi's listing platforms rarely publish more than one to two weeks ahead. Where a night is listed as recurring without a date, it is a standing weekly slot — check the group or venue before setting off.
